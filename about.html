About Me

Hello! I’m Ramavath Sai Kumar, a passionate and dedicated learner with a strong foundation in academics and technology. I was born and raised in Achampet, where I completed my schooling from 1st to 10th grade, achieving a CGPA of 9.2. Growing up in a town that values education, I developed a curiosity for learning and a disciplined approach to studies from an early age.

In 2016, I moved to Hyderabad to pursue my intermediate studies at Narayana Junior College, where I focused on strengthening my knowledge in mathematics, science, and analytical skills. The competitive environment helped me develop perseverance, problem-solving abilities, and a strong work ethic—qualities that have guided me throughout my academic and professional journey.

After months of dedicated preparation, I successfully cleared the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) in 2018, which allowed me to secure admission at IIT Varanasi, one of India’s premier institutes for engineering and technology. At IIT Varanasi, I pursued higher education with a focus on Machine Learning and AI, where I engaged in rigorous coursework, hands-on projects, and research initiatives. These experiences not only expanded my technical expertise but also nurtured my creativity, critical thinking, and passion for solving complex problems.

I am driven by curiosity, continuous learning, and the desire to make a meaningful impact in the world through technology and innovation. Beyond academics, I enjoy exploring new tools, working on projects, and collaborating with like-minded individuals to bring ideas to life.
